Data protection

Privacy Policy

This concise notice describes the processing that can be identified from the current website code. It is not a guarantee of compliance.

Controller and contact

Thomas Castañeda, Lissagasse 2/10, 1030 Wien, Austria, is the website operator. For privacy questions or rights requests, contact t.castaneda@me.com.

Hosting and access data

If this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical access information, such as IP address, browser/device information and request data, for security and operation. The provider and its current privacy, transfer and retention terms should be checked against the selected hosting arrangement before publication. This page does not state a retention period or contractual relationship that has not been verified.

Legal basis and any international-transfer safeguards depend on the hosting setup and should be documented by the operator.

Newsletter

The signup form sends the email address directly to the existing Mailchimp audience. The newsletter is intended to be sent approximately four times a year. Mailchimp may process subscriber data as a service provider and may transfer it internationally; its current privacy, transfer and processor terms apply.

The visible form currently contains only a required email field and a bot-protection honeypot. It does not, by itself, prove that a GDPR consent checkbox, double opt-in, tracking, retention control or particular legal basis is enabled. The operator must verify these settings in Mailchimp. Subscribers should be able to withdraw consent and unsubscribe at any time using the link in each message; requests for access or deletion may be sent to the contact above.

YouTube video

The home page eagerly loads the existing video from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced host and requests muted autoplay. This connects to YouTube/Google when the page loads, and they may process IP address, device and usage information under their own terms and policies. Autoplay may be limited by the visitor’s browser.

External links and email

The site has ordinary links to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube/YouTube Music, Bandcamp, Instagram and Facebook. Following a link takes the visitor to that provider, which may process data under its own policy. These links are not embedded players. The email link opens the visitor’s email application; the site has no server-side contact form.

Cookies and rights

The current first-party code contains no analytics, tracking pixels, localStorage or sessionStorage, and no external font request. No cookie banner has been added because no non-essential cookie or comparable terminal access was found in the active implementation. The eager YouTube embed should be reviewed against Austrian TKG 2021 § 174 and applicable EU rules if the implementation changes.

Subject to the GDPR, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability where applicable, objection where applicable, and withdrawal of consent. You may complain to a competent data protection supervisory authority. The appropriate lead authority is not determined here because the owner lives in Sweden while maintaining Austrian self-employed registration and an Austrian business address.

Sources and open checks

Relevant official sources include the GDPR, Austrian RIS, the Austrian Data Protection Authority, and the current privacy terms of GitHub and Mailchimp. Confirm the actual hosting account, Mailchimp settings, transfer safeguards, retention, tracking and supervisory jurisdiction before publication.
