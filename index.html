About

Originally from the Alps and now based in Stockholm, Thomas Castañeda is a pianist, composer and producer. After many years in Vienna, his musical approach was shaped in large part through ensemble work, particularly as co-founder of Donauwellenreiter. He co-produced all six of the ensemble’s albums and composed much of its repertoire, touring internationally and performing at festivals and venues across Europe, the Middle East and America.

His work spans neo-classical music, improvisation and interdisciplinary projects, including collaborations with Austrian philosopher Konrad Paul Liessmann.
